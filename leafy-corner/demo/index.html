Leafy Corner Demo

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Yes — Leafy Corner has a free demo on Steam. Steam lists the Leafy Corner Demo with a release date of April 17, 2026.

Demo confirmation here is based on the official Steam demo app page and Fireline Games’ Steam community announcement. Console store pages reviewed for this site list the full game, not a separate demo claim.
Demo Platform Steam (confirmed)
Demo Release Date April 17, 2026 (Steam listing)
Full Game Release July 30, 2026
Price Free demo; full game base price $9.99 USD

Where to get the demo

Official Steam demo page: store.steampowered.com/app/4534800/Leafy_Corner_Demo/

The full game page on Steam also links to the demo download section.

What the demo includes (official notes)

Fireline Games’ Steam announcement described demo access to:

  • Growing plants and discovering variegations
  • Helping customers find the right plants
  • Completing tasks to unlock plants, pots, and decorations
  • Building shop reputation
  • Character customization and shop decoration

The same announcement said more content awaits in the full game, including 100+ plants, orders, and shop expansion.

Console demo status

This guide does not claim a Nintendo Switch or PlayStation 5 demo. The Switch and PlayStation product pages used as sources list the full Leafy Corner game. If a console demo appears later, verify it on the official storefront before assuming availability.
