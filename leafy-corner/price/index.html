Leafy Corner Price

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Fireline Games announced a base launch price of $9.99 USD for Leafy Corner. Steam, Nintendo, and Xbox listings reviewed for this guide align with that base price in USD markets, with temporary launch discounts appearing on some storefronts.

Announced Base Price $9.99 USD
Steam (around launch) $9.99 with a temporary introductory discount (example: 20% off)
Xbox Store (USD listing) $9.99
Nintendo eShop Base price commonly listed at $9.99 USD; temporary sales may apply
Soundtrack (Steam) Original Soundtrack listed at $1.99

What official materials said before launch

Ahead of release, Fireline Games published regional Steam pricing examples tied to the $9.99 USD base (for example Europe €9.99 and UK £8.49 in the studio’s price announcement). Those figures were regional Steam examples, not a guarantee of every console store price in every territory.

Launch discounts

After launch, Steam showed an introductory offer on Leafy Corner (20% off the $9.99 USD price during the sale window). Nintendo’s US product page also indicated a limited-time sale period. Discounts expire, so treat any sale number as temporary.

Extra content pricing

Steam lists a Leafy Corner Original Soundtrack for $1.99. Steam also shows optional bundles that pair Leafy Corner with other games; those are separate store promotions, not required purchases.

What is not confirmed here

  • Exact live PS5 regional prices at the moment you visit this page
  • Whether every platform currently has the same discount percentage
  • Future permanent price changes after launch promotions end

Always confirm the current price on the storefront for your platform and country.

FAQ

This guide sticks to published pricing facts. For value judgment, check recent Steam reviews and the free Steam demo if available in your region.

Steam lists an Original Soundtrack. This page does not invent additional story or content DLC packs beyond what store pages show.
