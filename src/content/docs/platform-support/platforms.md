## What platforms is Leafy Corner on?

Leafy Corner is available on these officially listed storefronts:

- Steam (PC/macOS)
- Nintendo Switch
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox

Fireline Games also announced **Steam Deck Verified** status. See the [Leafy Corner release date](/leafy-corner/release-date/) for launch timing across these platforms.

Store pages can differ by region and can update independently. Check the relevant storefront before purchasing.

## Is Leafy Corner on PS5?

Yes. Leafy Corner is available on PlayStation 5 via the official PlayStation Store listing.

It launched on PS5 with the other officially listed storefronts; see the [release date guide](/leafy-corner/release-date/) for the confirmed date.
