## About Leafy Corner

**Leafy Corner** is a cozy plant-shop management game from Fireline Games. The official store descriptions focus on growing, selling, and caring for real-life plants, helping customers, completing orders, building shop reputation, and decorating the shop.

The Guides below separate gameplay, platform, release, price, and demo questions. Price and sale information is explicitly time-sensitive, while the gameplay page records the official scope boundaries.
