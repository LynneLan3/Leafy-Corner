## What kind of game is Leafy Corner?

Leafy Corner is a cozy plant-shop management game. The official store descriptions focus on growing, selling, and caring for real-life plants while helping customers and completing orders.

## Confirmed features

The reviewed listings describe:

- 100+ plants and plant variegations
- Growing, selling, and caring for plants
- Customer orders and tasks
- Shop decoration
- Reputation progression
- Character customization

The official demo description also says the game does **not** offer hardcore management, advanced plant care, or a storyline. This page therefore avoids presenting Leafy Corner as a deep simulation or story campaign.
