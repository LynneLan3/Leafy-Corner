What is Leafy Corner?

Leafy Corner is a cozy shop management game from Polish indie studio Fireline Games. You run a plant shop: grow and care for houseplants, help customers find the right plants, complete orders, and decorate your store.

Official store copy positions it as a relaxing, low-pressure experience. Fireline Games also states what the game does not include: hardcore management systems, advanced plant care simulation, or a storyline.

Gameplay

The core loop focuses on growing plants, serving customers with plant requests, fulfilling orders, unlocking decorations, and building shop reputation.

  • Grow, care for, and sell 100+ unique plants
  • Discover plant variegations
  • Decorate with pots, furniture, and other unlockable items
  • Complete tasks and raise shop reputation
  • Customize your character
