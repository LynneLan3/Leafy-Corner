# Leafy Corner SEO research

Research date: 2026-08-11

## Confirmed identity and facts

- **Developer / publisher:** Fireline Games.
- **Release:** July 30, 2026 across the officially listed Steam, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and Xbox storefronts.
- **Game identity:** a cozy plant-shop management game focused on growing, selling, and caring for real-life plants, helping customers, completing orders, and decorating the shop.
- **Gameplay scope:** official store features describe 100+ plants, variegations, shop decoration, customer orders/tasks, reputation progression, and character customization. Official demo copy also says the game does not offer hardcore management, advanced plant care, or a storyline.
- **Demo:** a free Steam demo is listed with an April 17, 2026 release date. The reviewed console product pages do not establish separate console demos.
- **Price:** Fireline Games announced a $9.99 USD base price with a limited launch discount; current regional and temporary prices must be checked on the live storefront.
- **Steam Deck:** Fireline Games announced Steam Deck Verified status.

## Intentionally omitted

No plant-by-plant database, optimization/build guide, detailed shop economy, DLC catalog, or console-demo page is added. Official sources do not support a stable exhaustive catalog for those topics, and price/sale claims remain explicitly regional and time-sensitive.

## Migration decision

Leafy Corner already has target-specific static Hub + Guide architecture, unique metadata, source links, and no generic template residue. Public URLs are preserved; this migration records the evidence boundary and validates the current implementation without speculative expansion.
